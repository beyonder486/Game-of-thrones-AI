Algorithm 4 · A* Pathfinding

The safest road to the throne

f(n) = g(n) + h(n) · g = Σ defense bonuses crossed so far · h = hex distance left to the goal

🎯 Used for

Minimax throne bonus 1.5 × max(0, 12 − cost) · a human click on a distant hold marches 1 step along the route.

❓ Why A*

Cost so far + a distance estimate points the search at the goal, so it explores far fewer holds than a blind search.
