💡 Key Insight

The winner is checked after the whole round, so rivals later in the round get one last chance to retake the throne.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Allies never share gold or armies. An alliance is only a non-aggression pact.

The randomized turn order keeps the game fair; in 8 simulated games the wins split 3 / 3 / 2.
