Algorithm 6 · Minimax + Alpha-Beta

How the AI scores a board

V = 2·Σ(r + b) + 1.5·Armies + 0.5·Gold + 500·[own King’s Landing] + 1.5·max(0, 12 − A* cost to throne) − Σ enemies (Σ r + 1.5·Armies)

TermWeightMeaning
Σ (r + b) of my holds× 2land: income + defense
My armies× 1.5military
My gold× 0.5treasury
Own King’s Landing+ 500the throne
max(0, 12 − A* cost)× 1.5closer to the throne
Σ enemies (Σ r + 1.5·armies)−enemy power

❓ Why Minimax

A move must survive the enemy’s best reply. Alpha-beta: same answer, faster.

💡 Key Insight

A* feeds V directly: every step closer to the throne is worth up to +1.5 per cost point saved.
