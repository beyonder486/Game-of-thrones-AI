Algorithm 1 · Breadth-First Search

What can we reach in 3 marches?

📥 Takes

map, capital, n_steps = 3

⚙️ Calculates

queue, ring by ring: 1, 2, 3 steps, never twice

📤 Returns

reachable holds → house.reachable_holds

❓ Why BFS

Everything within a fixed number of steps, fewest operations, no weights needed.

🔎 Technical Note

Queue (FIFO), O(V + E), shortest step counts.

⚠️ Mistake

BFS/DFS are scouting info; they don’t change Minimax or MCTS.
