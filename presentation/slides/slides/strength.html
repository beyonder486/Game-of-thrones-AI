Attacker versus defender

⚔️ Attacker

Attack power = armies × luck

Only its army count matters.

VS

luck 0.80 – 1.20
rolled per side

Strictly greater wins

tie → defender holds

🛡️ Defender

Neutral: troops × bonus
House: armies × (1 + 0.05 × bonus)

Defense power = defense × luck

King’s Landing 11 × 3 = 33

Neutral 4 × 3 = 12

House 20 × 1.10 = 22

❓ Why two formulas

Garrisons are small (2–11): full bonus. Houses defend with 14–40 armies: +5% per level, or wars never happen.

The defense bonus comes from the hold being attacked, not the defender’s capital. Sprites are the game’s own Medieval Warrior and Evil Wizard animations.
