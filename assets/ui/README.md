# Snow Grounds Vol.1

4 seamless snow ground textures: fresh powder and packed snow, realistic and stylized. Tileable PNG, game-ready.

## Contents

- `snow_real_s402_seamless.png` — realistic snow ground
- `snow_styl_s502_seamless.png` — stylized snow ground
- `snow_fresh_s42.png` — snow ground, fresh variant
- `snow_packed_s42.png` — snow ground, packed variant

All textures are seamless/tileable PNG files. Drop them into your engine of
choice (Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Tiled...) and tile freely.
