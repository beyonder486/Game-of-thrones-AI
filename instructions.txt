=======================================================================
GAME OF THRONES: AI CONQUEST — COMPLETE GAME MECHANICS & AI ARCHITECTURE
=======================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & CORE CONCEPT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
"Game of Thrones: AI Conquest" is a turn-based 2.5D isometric strategy
game set on a hex-grid map of Westeros. Three Great Houses — STARK,
LANNISTER, and TARGARYEN — compete for the Iron Throne through
territorial conquest, economy management, military planning, and
emergent political diplomacy (alliances, trust, and betrayal).

Supported Modes:
  - AI vs AI (Observer Mode): All 3 houses are controlled by the 7-algorithm
    AI stack. Viewers step turns manually via "ADVANCE TURN >>" or
    SPACE / ENTER / RIGHT ARROW.
  - Human vs AI (Player Command): The player commands one Great House
    (Stark, Lannister, or Targaryen) while the other two are driven by AI.

Win Conditions (checked after every combat and at end of round):
  1. Iron Throne Victory: Capture KING'S LANDING (instant win).
  2. Total Elimination Victory: Be the last surviving house with >= 1 hold.

Turn Order:
  - At the start of every new game, turn order among the 3 houses is
    shuffled randomly (`random.shuffle(self.houses)`) so no house holds
    a permanent first-mover advantage.


=======================================================================
2. CRITICAL DESIGN RULES
=======================================================================
1. One military action per house per turn (Attack a territory, Betray +
   strike, or Hold fortifications).
2. Diplomatic proposals are asynchronous: sending a proposal queues it
   in the receiver's inbox (`house.inbox`) and does NOT consume the
   proposer's military move for that turn. The receiver evaluates it on
   their next turn.
3. Betrayal is unilateral — no proposal or consent is required.
4. No resource or army sharing between allied houses. Captured holds
   and eliminated-house gold go strictly to the house that physically
   captures the territory.
5. Eliminated houses (0 territories) are immediately stripped of active
   alliances and all pending proposals to/from them are purged. Alliances
   cannot be proposed or formed once fewer than 3 houses remain alive.
6. Strict AI Layer Separation:
     - Fuzzy Logic outputs continuous desirability scores (0.00–1.00) ONLY;
       it never makes a final decision.
     - MCTS makes ALL final diplomatic decisions (using Fuzzy scores as
       UCT prior biases).
     - Greedy is used both as a standalone baseline and as the deterministic
       opponent rollout model inside MCTS (never random rollouts).
     - Minimax + A* make ALL military/combat decisions (including when to
       Hold). Hold is a military decision, NOT a Fuzzy diplomacy output.

3. MAP, STARTING STATE, ECONOMY & SUPPLY CAP
=======================================================================
Map Geometry (`map.py`):
  - 21 x 8 offset hex grid containing 19 named territories across Westeros.
  - Each land hex is assigned to its nearest territory seat (Voronoi
    regions), and the territory adjacency graph (`Territory.neighbors`) is
    derived strictly from touching hex regions on the map.
  - Each territory has:
      * resource_value : Gold generated per round
      * defense_bonus  : Fortification level (1 to 4)
      * armies         : Neutral garrison size (when unowned)
      * owner          : "STARK", "LANNISTER", "TARGARYEN", or None

Starting Values (`constants.py`, `map.py`):
  - HOUSE STARK:     60 Gold, 14 Armies, Seat: WINTERFELL    (res=9, def=3)
  - HOUSE LANNISTER: 65 Gold, 14 Armies, Seat: CASTERLY ROCK (res=9, def=2)
  - HOUSE TARGARYEN: 60 Gold, 14 Armies, Seat: DRAGONSTONE   (res=8, def=4)
  - IRON THRONE:     KING'S LANDING (neutral, res=15, def=3, garrison=11)
                     -> Neutral effective defense = 11 * 3 = 33.
  - All other 15 holds start neutral with 2–4 garrison armies and def 1–3.
  - Initial pairwise trust between every pair of houses = 50.0 / 100.0.

End-of-Round Economy & Supply Limit (`main.py:_end_of_round`):
  After all surviving houses have acted in a round:
  1. Income Collection:
       income = BASE_INCOME (10) + sum(t.resource_value for owned holds)
  2. Automatic Army Recruitment:
       - ARMY_COST = 5 Gold per army
       - Reserve floor = 15 Gold (houses keep at least 15G in reserve)
       - Per-round recruitment cap = MAX_RECRUITS_PER_TURN (3 armies)
       - Realm Army Supply Cap = 10 + 4 * (number of owned holds)
       - Formula:
           recruits = min(
               3,
               max(0, (gold - 15) // 5),
               max(0, (10 + 4 * owned_holds) - armies)
           )
     This supply cap prevents infinite army stockpiling by repeatedly
     pressing "HOLD TURN" and requires owning at least 6 holds (34 cap)
     to field an army strong enough to take King's Landing (33 defense).
  3. Trust Update:
       Surviving alliances gain +ALLY_BONUS (+5 trust) per round.
  4. Context A Fuzzy Refresh & Win Condition Check:
       Recomputes Fuzzy diplomacy telemetry and advances the turn counter.

4. COMPLETE TURN PIPELINE (`main.py`)
=======================================================================
For each surviving house in the shuffled turn order:

A. AI House Turn (`step_house_turn`):
  IF house.inbox has a pending proposal from a surviving proposer:
    1. Fuzzy Logic (Context B) — evaluate alliance/betrayal/solo scores
       using the 3rd (common enemy) house's strength, receiver's own
       weakness, and trust toward the proposer.
    2. MCTS (Context B)        — simulate "accept" vs "reject" futures.
    3. Resolve Proposal        — accept (form alliance -> PARANOID mode)
                                 or reject (clear inbox, log rejection).
    4. BFS / DFS               — update `house.reachable_holds` (2 steps)
                                 and `house.expansion_paths` (depth 3).
    5. Minimax (Alpha-Beta)    — select best adjacent attack or Hold (None).
    6. A* Routing & Combat     — resolve battle if target selected.

  ELSE (no pending proposal):
    1. BFS / DFS               — update `house.reachable_holds` and
                                 `house.expansion_paths`.
    2. Fuzzy Logic (Context A) — evaluate diplomatic landscape against all
                                 active rivals.
    3. MCTS (Context A)        — choose "propose_alliance", "betray",
                                 or "solo".
       - If "propose_alliance": queue proposal in target.inbox (house
         still performs its military move this same turn).
       - If "betray": break alliance (-30 trust), log betrayal, and pass
         `betray_target=ally` to Minimax for an immediate strike.
    4. Minimax (Alpha-Beta)    — select best adjacent attack or Hold (None).
    5. A* Routing & Combat     — resolve battle if target selected.

B. Human Player Turn (`handle_human_action`):
  - On turn start, the controller resets `_human_proposed_this_turn = False`,
    refreshes Fuzzy scores (Context B if inbox has a proposal, else Context A),
    and precomputes `human_minimax_scores` for live territory-hover previews.
  - If an inbox proposal is pending: player clicks ACCEPT or REJECT first
    (does not end turn; refreshes Minimax hover scores afterward).
  - Player may optionally send 1 alliance proposal (`ALLY <HOUSE>`) without
    ending their turn (blocked if < 3 houses survive or already proposed
    this turn).
  - Player concludes their turn with ONE military action:
      * Click an adjacent enemy/neutral territory to attack it directly.
      * Click a distant territory to march 1 border step along its lowest-
        defense A* route.
      * Click `BETRAY <ALLY>` to break an alliance and immediately launch
        Minimax's best strike against the former ally.
      * Click `HOLD TURN` to fortify and pass.

5. COMBAT MECHANICS & CASUALTY MODEL (`combat.py`)
=======================================================================
Effective Defense (`effective_defense`):
  - Neutral Garrison (small 2–10 force):
      base_def = garrison_armies * territory.defense_bonus
      (Example: King's Landing = 10 * 3 = 30.0)
  - Rival Great House (full army pool):
      base_def = defender.armies * (1.0 + 0.05 * territory.defense_bonus)
      (Provides +5% to +20% fortification bonus without freezing rival
      borders into a permanent standoff.)

Stochastic Battle Resolution (`Combat.resolve_combat`):
  attacker_power = attacker.armies * Uniform(0.80, 1.20)
  defender_power = base_def        * Uniform(0.80, 1.20)

  IF attacker_power > defender_power (Attacker Wins):
    - attacker_losses = int(defender_armies * 0.50)
    - defender_losses = int(defender_armies * 0.80)
    - Territory transfers to attacker.
    - If defender loses its final territory (eliminated):
        * All of defender's remaining gold transfers to the capturer.
        * `diplomacy.purge_eliminated(defender)` dissolves any active
          alliance and purges all pending proposals to/from defender.
  ELSE (Defender Holds / Attacker Repelled):
    - attacker_losses = int(attacker.armies * 0.60)
    - defender_losses = int(min(attacker.armies, defender_armies) * 0.20)
    - Territory remains with defender.

Army Floor:
  - Surviving armies on both sides are always clamped to `max(1, ...)`.

Expected Value vs. Live Variance:
  - Inside deterministic Minimax search, `Uniform(0.80, 1.20)` is
    approximated by its mean `1.0`, so `attacker > base_def` represents
    a > 50% win probability.
  - Because live combat rolls `±20%`, a narrow favorite can still be
    repelled on an unlucky roll. A 100% guaranteed win regardless of RNG
    requires `attacker.armies > 1.5 * base_def` (since 1.2 / 0.8 = 1.5).

6. DIPLOMACY, ALLIANCES, TRUST & BETRAYAL (`diplomacy.py`)
=======================================================================
What an Active Alliance Does:
  1. Strict Non-Aggression: Minimax places the active ally in
     `my_forbidden`, so an allied AI will NEVER attack its ally without
     first choosing MCTS betrayal (even if surrounded only by ally holds,
     it chooses Hold instead).
  2. Coordinated Focus Against the 3rd House: Both allies direct their
     separate turns against neutral holds and the 3rd house.
  3. Paranoid Minimax Mode Sync: Forming an alliance immediately switches
     ALL surviving houses (both allies AND the lone 3rd house) to
     `PARANOID` mode. Dissolving the alliance resets all houses to
     `DECOMPOSED` mode.
  4. Trust Growth: Every round the alliance holds adds `+5` trust
     (`ALLY_BONUS`) to the pair (clamped to 0–100).
  5. No Resource or Army Sharing: Gold, income, troops, and captured
     territories remain completely separate.

Alliance Formation (2-Turn Asynchronous Handshake):
  - Turn N: Proposer calls `diplomacy.queue_proposal(proposer, target, N)`.
    Proposal `{"from": proposer, "type": "alliance_proposal", "turn": N}`
    is placed in `target.inbox`. Proposer still makes its military move.
  - Turn N+1: Receiver evaluates the proposal (Fuzzy Context B + MCTS
    Context B for AI; ACCEPT/REJECT buttons for Human).
    * Accept -> `form_alliance(proposer, target, turn)`
    * Reject -> clear inbox and log rejection.

Three Ways Betrayal Occurs (All Apply -30 `BETRAYAL_PENALTY` & Log):
  1. Explicit Betrayal: AI MCTS chooses `"betray"`, or Human clicks
     `BETRAY <ALLY>`. Breaks pact, subtracts 30 trust, and immediately
     strikes the former ally on the same turn.
  2. Attacking an Ally Directly (Human): Clicking an ally's hold in
     `_execute_attack()` automatically triggers `break_alliance()` (-30
     trust + betrayal log) right before combat resolves.
  3. Switching Allies Automatically: Accepting a new alliance with House C
     while already allied with House B inside `form_alliance()`
     automatically betrays House B (-30 trust + `"betrayed B to ally with C"`).

7. THE 7 AI ALGORITHMS & HOW THEY FUNCTION TOGETHER
=======================================================================

ALGORITHM 1 — BFS (`algorithms/bfs.py`)
  - Function: `get_reachable(game_map, start, n_steps) -> set[Territory]`
  - Role: Breadth-first traversal finding all territories reachable within
    `n_steps` graph hops (run at `n=2` each turn and persisted on
    `house.reachable_holds`). Used for proximity and reachability checks.

ALGORITHM 2 — DFS (`algorithms/dfs.py`)
  - Function: `get_expansion_paths(game_map, start, depth_limit) -> list[list[Territory]]`
  - Role: Depth-first backtracking search discovering all simple (cycle-free)
    corridors up to `depth_limit=3` into neutral or lightly defended
    (`defense_bonus < 2`) territories. Persisted on `house.expansion_paths`.

ALGORITHM 3 — GREEDY (`algorithms/greedy.py`)
  - Functions: `greedy_pick(candidates)` and `get_greedy_move(house, game_map, enemies)`
  - Formula: `argmax(resource_value - defense_bonus)` with ties favoring
    `KING'S LANDING`.
  - Dual Role:
      1. Standalone heuristic target selector.
      2. Opponent & player rollout policy inside MCTS (`mcts.py` imports
         `greedy_pick` directly so every simulated turn in MCTS uses Greedy
         decisions rather than random moves).

ALGORITHM 4 — A* PATHFINDING (`algorithms/astar.py`)
  - Function: `get_path(game_map, start, goal) -> list[Territory]`
  - Cost g(n): Sum of `defense_bonus` of each entered territory (lowest-risk
    multi-turn corridor).
  - Heuristic h(n): `hex_distance((a.col, a.row), (b.col, b.row))` using
    cube-coordinate hex distance (`map.py:hex_distance`).
  - Dual Role:
      1. AI Minimax Evaluation: `_throne_path_costs()` caches the A*
         defense-weighted path cost from every hold to `KING'S LANDING`
         and rewards AI moves that advance along the cheapest A* corridor.
      2. Human Multi-Turn Routing: Clicking any distant non-adjacent hold
         plots the optimal A* path and automatically attacks the first
         border territory along that route.

ALGORITHM 5 — MAMDANI FUZZY LOGIC (`algorithms/fuzzy_diplomacy.py`)
  - Function: `evaluate_diplomacy(enemy_strength, own_weakness, trust_history) -> dict`
  - Inputs (all 0–100): `enemy_strength`, `own_weakness`, `trust_history`.
  - Outputs (all 0.00–1.00): `{"alliance": float, "betrayal": float, "solo": float}`.
  - Membership Functions: Triangular (`trimf`):
      * LOW    = (0, 0, 60)
      * MEDIUM = (20, 50, 80)
      * HIGH   = (40, 100, 100)
  - Inference: Mamdani min-implication, max-aggregation, centroid
    defuzzification (uses `scikit-fuzzy` if installed, pure-NumPy fallback
    otherwise).
  - 6 Linguistic Rules:
      R1: enemy HIGH & weakness HIGH      -> alliance HIGH
      R2: trust LOW  & enemy MEDIUM       -> alliance LOW
      R3: weakness LOW & enemy LOW        -> betrayal HIGH
      R4: weakness LOW & trust HIGH       -> solo HIGH
      R5: enemy HIGH & trust HIGH         -> alliance MEDIUM
      R6: trust LOW                       -> betrayal MEDIUM
  - Two Runtime Contexts:
      * Context A (Own Diplomacy): `enemy_strength` = combined enemies,
        `own_weakness` = 100 - scaled own strength, `trust` = avg enemy trust.
      * Context B (Incoming Proposal): `enemy_strength` = 3rd (common enemy)
        house's strength when alive (fallback: proposer), `own_weakness` =
        receiver weakness, `trust` = trust toward proposer.

ALGORITHM 6 — MINIMAX WITH ALPHA-BETA PRUNING (`algorithms/minimax.py`)
  - Functions: `get_move_scores(...) -> dict[str|None, float]` and
    `get_best_move(...) -> Territory | None`.
  - Depth Limit: `MINIMAX_DEPTH = 3`, with Alpha-Beta pruning.
  - Evaluation Function (`_evaluate_state`):
      score = (own_territory_score * 2)
            + (own_armies * 1.5)
            + (own_gold * 0.5)
            + THRONE_BONUS (500 if owns King's Landing)
            + A*_throne_approach (max(0, 12 - min_astar_cost) * 1.5)
            - sum(enemy_territory_resources + enemy_armies * 1.5)
  - Two Search Modes:
      1. DECOMPOSED Mode (no active alliance):
         Evaluates each opponent independently and combines via military-
         strength weights:
           score = w2 * minimax(Me vs H2) + w3 * minimax(Me vs H3)
      2. PARANOID Mode (any active alliance):
         Merges opposing houses into a single minimizing coalition that
         never attacks itself and always chooses the worst reply for Me.
  - Candidate Filters (`_moves`):
      * Never attacks own holds or active ally (`my_forbidden`).
      * Army Sufficiency Restraint: if `armies < MIN_ATTACK_ARMIES (5)`,
        only considers neutral territories.
      * Iron Throne Guard: skips `KING'S LANDING` unless
        `armies >= effective_defense(...)`.
  - Opening Variety (`_pick`): Randomly breaks ties among attacks within
    `2.0` points of the optimal score so AI games develop unique borders.
  - Live Human Preview: `get_move_scores()` powers the tooltip preview
    (`Minimax: +XX.X · BEST` vs `Hold`) when hovering over adjacent holds.

ALGORITHM 7 — MONTE CARLO TREE SEARCH / UCT (`algorithms/mcts.py`)
  - Functions:
      * Context A: `get_diplomacy_decision(...) -> ("propose_alliance"|"betray"|"solo", target)`
      * Context B: `get_proposal_response(...)  -> "accept" | "reject"`
  - Parameters: `MCTS_ITER = 500`, `MCTS_ROLLOUT_DEPTH = 10`, `UCT_C = 1.414`.
  - UCT Selection Formula (with Fuzzy prior bias):
      UCT = (Q / N) + UCT_C * sqrt(ln(N_parent) / N) + fuzzy_bias[action]
      * Context A biases: propose_alliance <- alliance, betray <- betrayal,
        solo <- solo. Partner selection prefers highest trust, breaking ties
        toward the weaker rival (so two weaker houses unite against the
        strongest).
      * Context B biases: accept <- alliance, reject <- max(betrayal, solo).
  - Rollout Engine (`_Sim` + `_rollout`):
      * All houses pick moves with `greedy_pick()` (never random moves).
      * Uses real stochastic combat rolls `Uniform(0.80, 1.20)`, `effective_defense`,
        `>= 1` army floor, Iron Throne threshold guard, `15G` reserve,
        `MAX_RECRUITS_PER_TURN = 3`, and supply cap `12 + 6 * holds`.
      * Alliance Breakdown: after step 0, each turn has breakdown probability
        `p_break = (1 - trust / 100) * 0.25`; if it breaks, alliance ends
        and `+10.0` risk penalty is added.
  - Reward Function (scaled by `REWARD_SCALE = 100.0`):
      * Captures Iron Throne -> `1000 / 100`
      * Eliminated           -> `0.0`
      * Otherwise:
          0.5 * territory_score + 0.3 * military_strength
        + 0.2 * resource_score - 0.4 * alliance_risk_penalty
        (halved if a rival captures the Iron Throne first).


=======================================================================
8. ALGORITHM INTERACTION CHAINS
=======================================================================
Chain 1 — Own Diplomacy (Context A):
  Fuzzy(Context A) -> UCT prior bias -> MCTS(Context A, Greedy rollouts)
  -> propose_alliance (queue inbox) | betray (break pact + strike) | solo

Chain 2 — Incoming Proposal (Context B):
  Fuzzy(Context B, 3rd-house threat) -> UCT prior bias -> MCTS(Context B)
  -> accept (form alliance, switch all houses to PARANOID) | reject

Chain 3 — Military Expansion & Combat:
  BFS/DFS (reachability & corridors) + A* (throne-corridor cost)
  -> Minimax (Decomposed or Paranoid, Alpha-Beta) -> target selection
  -> A* (first border step for multi-hop human clicks) -> Combat resolution

Chain 4 — Elimination Cleanup:
  Defender reaches 0 holds -> gold looted by capturer
  -> `purge_eliminated()` dissolves orphaned pact & clears all stale
     inbox proposals -> remaining 2 houses fight to completion without
     dead-house proposals or 2-player alliances.

Known Approximations:
  1. Decomposed Minimax evaluates rival houses independently using
     strength-weighted sums, which may underestimate unallied coordination.
  2. Paranoid Minimax assumes full coalition cooperation while an alliance
     holds; MCTS models probabilistic alliance breakdown over future turns.
  3. Minimax evaluates combat using expected roll `1.0` for speed, whereas
     live combat and MCTS rollouts sample `Uniform(0.80, 1.20)`.
